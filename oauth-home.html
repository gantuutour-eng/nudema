Nudema in Mongolia

App purpose: Nudema in Mongolia is an online store that helps customers in Mongolia browse and purchase skincare and beauty products.

What the app does

Customers can view product information, compare prices and reviews, place orders for delivery across Mongolia, and track their order history.

How Google user data is used

Google Sign-In is used only to create or access a customer account. The app uses the customer's basic Google account information, such as name and email address, to identify the account, process orders, and display order history. It does not access Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, calendars, or other Google services.
